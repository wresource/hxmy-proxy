<en-US>
• Added UDP forwarding, SOCKS5 BIND and phone DNS/Echo interfaces for compatible clients.
• Improved IPv4/IPv6 selection for direct TCP connections and reduced delays from slow DNS queries.
• Added “Allow local network targets”, off by default. PAC bypasses local addresses and names; forced local requests show bypass guidance.
• Preserved existing rules, traffic views and outgoing-network settings. No root or additional VPN service is required.
</en-US>

<zh-CN>
